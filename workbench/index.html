A SMALL FIRST STEP, BUILT PRECISELY

Meet your workbench.

An experimental local CAD workspace for dimensioned solids. B-CAD is beginning its journey toward a complete parametric CAD environment.

  1. 01
    Start with a part

    Create a box, a drilled plate or a cylinder. You can also import an exact solid from a STEP file.

  2. 02
    Give it dimensions

    Use millimeters, explicit units like 2 in, or an expression like width / 2. Preview, then apply the change.

  3. 03
    Keep it, or take it with you

    Save an editable .bcad project. Export exact STEP geometry or a millimeter-based STL mesh.
